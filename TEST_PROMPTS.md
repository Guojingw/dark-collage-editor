# Smoke-test prompts

Use these after installing the plugin.

## Single portrait / default

Upload one portrait and say:

> 用 Dark Collage Editor 修这张。Editorial，不要文字。保留我的脸、动作和原始横竖构图。

Expected behavior: one dominant portrait, controlled red/black/off-white grunge, no invented readable copy, no wall of repeated faces.

## Custom text

> Editorial。主文字写 STAY IN THE NOISE，只出现一次，不要挡脸。

Expected behavior: exact wording; typography is secondary to the subject.

## Batch

Upload 4–9 portraits and say:

> 做成同一组，统一色调和材质，但每张版式不同。不要通过增加重复人脸来制造“混乱”。

Expected behavior: series consistency, composition variation, sparse source-photo cutouts.

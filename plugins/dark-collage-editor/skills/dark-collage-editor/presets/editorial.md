# Editorial preset

Default preset.

- Main portrait: 70–85% visual attention
- Portrait fragments: 1–2 maximum
- Torn paper: high
- Graffiti: medium
- Red accents: medium
- Typography: medium
- Grain / photocopy texture: medium to high
- Negative space: medium
- Composition: magazine / music-zine / art-poster feeling
- Avoid symmetrical layouts and repeated templates
